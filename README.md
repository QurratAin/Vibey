# Vibey 🎵 — Ambient Sound Mixer for Focus, Sleep & Relaxation

**Soundscapes for every moment**

Vibey is a **free, lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA)** that lets you create custom ambient sound mixes right in your browser. Mix rain, ocean waves, fireplace crackles, white noise, and more to craft your perfect sonic environment — no downloads, no ads, no sign-ups.

🔗 **Try it now:** [https://qurratain.github.io/Vibey/]

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎚️ Mix & Match Sounds** — Combine multiple ambient sounds with individual volume controls
- **⏱️ Sleep Timer** — Set a timer that gradually fades sounds to silence
- **💾 Save Your Mixes** — Save up to 3 custom sound combinations for quick access
- **📱 PWA Ready** — Install on your phone or desktop for a native-like experience
- **🌐 Works Offline** — Downloaded sounds play even without an internet connection
- **🎨 Beautiful Dark UI** — Easy on the eyes, perfect for sleep or late-night focus sessions
- **⚡ Lightweight & Fast** — No heavy frameworks, no bloat, just pure audio


## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open [Vibey](https://qurratain.github.io/Vibey/) in your browser
2. Go to the **Downloads** tab and download the sounds you want
3. Switch to the **Sounds** tab and add sounds to your mix
4. Adjust individual volumes with the sliders
5. Set a sleep timer if desired
6. Save your favorite mixes for later

## 📲 Install as an App

**Chrome / Edge (Desktop & Android):** Click the "Install" button in the address bar

**Safari (iPhone / iPad):** Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"

**Firefox (Android):** Menu → "Install"
